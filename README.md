## The entry

Exactly one word describing the boss:

**Hovercraft**

A boss who hovers over your shoulder all day — the word lands on its own:
funny on its own, true of every hovering boss, fresh (not the safe pick),
and it travels to any workplace, office, kitchen or warehouse.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(UTF-8, 10 characters, one word, no spaces). Pure Python standard
library, no third-party imports.
